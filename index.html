Below includes a short description of each calculator and where to find them.

Basic Chemistry

  • Temperature Converter: Convert between Fahrenheit, Celcius and Kelvin.
  • Dilutions: Determine the volume or concentration of a solution before or after dilution.
  • Absorbance: Determine the absorbance of a solution given the concentration.
  • Molarity: Calculate the Molarity of a solution.

Acid/Base

  • pH Conversion: Convert between pH, pOH, Hydrogen Ion Concentration, and Hydroxide Ion Concentration.

Gas Laws

  • Boyle's Law: Determine the pressure or volume of a gas before or after changing the pressure or volume.
  • Charle's Law: Determine the temperature or volume of a gas before or after changing the temperature or volume.
  • Ideal Gas Law: Convert between pressure, temperature, mols, or volume of a gas at normal conditions.

Analytical Chemistry

  • Buoyancy Correction: Correct the mass of an object affected by the buoyancy of air.
  • Percent Error: Calculate the percentage error between the approximate value and exact value.
